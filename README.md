<h1>Hi, I'm Adil 👋</h1>

<h3>Think of it as my working notebook.</h3>

It's where I turn what I understand about **operations, supply chain, finance, research and design** into tech, starting with a scrappy **MVP** and sharpening it until it's **v1-ready** for the real world.

<br />

<h3>⚡ Lately, I've been working on</h3>

- **Agentic workflows** on n8n with tool calling, LLM decision nodes and API orchestration
- **Always-on agents** that monitor operations and act in the background
- **Voice agents** for outbound calls, follow-ups and customer conversations
- **Multi-agent systems** for research, sales intelligence and supply chain workflows
- **Evals, guardrails and observability**, the less exciting parts that make agents reliable
- **MCP integrations** and rapid prototyping with Claude Code and Cursor
